Push-to-talk dictation for macOS that never leaves your Mac.

Download for macOS

macOS 26 or later on Apple Silicon. Free and open source.

  • Hold a modifier key, speak, release. The text appears at the cursor in whatever app is in front.
  • Recognition runs on Apple's on-device speech models. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • Japanese and English, with a personal dictionary for the words it gets wrong.
